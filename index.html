Yang Li

 

I am an assistant professor of computer science at Iowa State University. Before that, I was a senior research scientist at Meta and a researcher at Microsoft. I received PhD and MS degrees from Carnegie Mellon University (advisor: Prof. José M. F. Moura, member of the National Academy of Engineering), an MSE degree from the University of Texas at Austin, and a BE degree from Tsinghua University. I am a recipient of the IBM Patent Application Award.

E-mail:  yangli1@iastate.edu; jerryyangli@gmail.com; yangli1@alumni.cmu.edu

Research Interests

Efficient AI and computer systems, including:

  • Model efficiency: LLM compression, including its effects on safety and agentic capabilities

  • Training efficiency: data selection, quality, and mixing for language models

  • Inference efficiency: long-context compression; efficient multimodal reasoning

  • System efficiency: model–system co-design; memory, cache, and power management for on-device AI and cloud systems
